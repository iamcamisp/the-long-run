The cost of keeping things runningO custo de manter tudo funcionando

The electricity bill and the price of borrowing are telling parts of the same story: keeping an economy running gets expensive when energy is scarce. Brazil has had a welcome breather, while the Fed has made credit dearer and AI companies are negotiating their place on the power grid.
